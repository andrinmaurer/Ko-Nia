# Block 2 proposal: Temple Hill (Old Town)

Status: proposal, waiting for Andrin's OK. Nothing is built yet.

## Why this block next

`13_CITY_AND_DISTRICTS.md` names Old Town's landmark as "a large temple complex (beyond the MVP block)". Shrine Street already ends at a small neighborhood shrine, so the temple is the natural next step: same district, same look and species, one step deeper into the mystery. It also adds the first **fungi**, **insects** and **water life**, which the Logbook doesn't have yet.

## How it connects to Shrine Street

- Behind the Neighborhood Shrine, a gap opens in the collapsed eastern building. A stone stair lane climbs about 8 m and bends once.
- Only one block is drawn at a time. The switch happens at the bend, where neither block is visible, so the frame rate stays what it is on Shrine Street today.
- From Shrine Street you can already see the top of the giant Temple Cedar over the rooftops, so the player has a reason to look for the way up.

## The block (about 120 m x 80 m)

- **Stair lane:** worn stone steps split by roots, stone lanterns on both sides, Rebar Ivy and Shutter Ferns carried over from Shrine Street.
- **Fallen main gate:** a huge wooden gate, half collapsed, that you walk under into the courtyard.
- **Courtyard:** gravel overgrown with moss, a fallen bronze temple bell, stone lanterns, offering racks.
- **Interior (the hall):** the big wooden main hall. Its roof caved in where the Temple Cedar grew through it. Dark, dusty, light in shafts. Home of the fungi and the danger.
- **Elevated route:** a leaning five-storey pagoda you can climb tier by tier (mantle), with a view over the courtyard and down into the hall through its broken roof.
- **Water:** the old koi pond has become a small wetland with a stone bridge.
- **Landmark:** the Temple Cedar, about 60 m tall, the tallest thing the player has seen.
- **Relay Station 2:** in the courtyard. Activating it saves, like the first one.
- Closed in by the hillside and collapsed walls, so again no invisible walls.

## New species

Shrine Street species (Hookbill, Lantern Moth, Rebar Ivy, Shutter Fern) also appear here but are already in the Logbook.

**Plants and fungi**
1. **Temple Cedar** (cedar, plant). The landmark tree. Its resin beads catch the light and glow amber at sunset. Completes when scanned at sunset. Cross-links to Gilded Carp (they eat its seeds).
2. **Scroll Cap** (fungus). Pale, layered caps that look like stacked paper slips, eating old wood and paper in the hall. Completes when scanned growing on a scroll shelf, its favourite food.
3. **Censer Puffball** (fungus, the danger, see below).

**Animals**
1. **Gilded Carp** (koi). Over a metre long, with scaly gold plates. Hides in the deep part of the pond. Completes when you stand still on the bridge until it surfaces to eat fallen cedar seeds.
2. **Tithe Ant** (ant). Columns of finger-sized ants that strip leftovers and carry them in neat lines. Completes when you follow a column back to its mound under the offering racks.
3. **Moss Tanuki** (raccoon dog). Its back grows moss as camouflage. It sleeps on the gate roof by day and forages at night, and it is curious: it follows the robot at a distance. Completes when you watch it raid the offering box at night.

## Danger: Censer Puffball

- Swollen ochre puffballs crowd the dark part of the hall, along the shortest way to the major discovery.
- **Warning:** when the robot gets close, they swell and hiss for 2 seconds. Walking slowly sets them off later than sprinting.
- **What it does:** they burst into a visible spore cloud. Spores clog the robot's sensors. The scanner goes offline and the view fogs for about 20 seconds, and it costs energy. It is never deadly, it gets in your way.
- **How to avoid it:** back off on the hiss, or take the long way over the pagoda and across the broken roof beams.
- **What you learn:** first scan from a distance creates the page. Watching a puffball burst from the pagoda (safe distance) completes it.

This is a different kind of danger from the Vault Mantis (a cloud you route around instead of a creature that strikes), which tests the "dangers are traversal problems" rule.

## Major discovery: the Wish Wall

At the back of the hall, behind the puffballs, is a wall of thousands of small wooden wish plaques, preserved by the dry air. Scanning it creates the first big **Human Artifacts** entry. Only a few plaques are readable, in fragments, and they hint at what happened without explaining it (for example one asks for "the water to go down", another for "the others to come back").

Small extra: at the top of the pagoda, a scratch mark left by the Predecessor Unit adds a second line to the Origin page ("it was here too, and it kept going"). It rewards the climb and points to the next block.
