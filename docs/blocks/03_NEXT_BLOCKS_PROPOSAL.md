# Blocks 3 to 5 proposal: leaving Old Town

Status: proposal, waiting for Andrin's OK. Nothing is built yet.

## Where we are

- Shrine Street and Temple Hill make up Old Town. Since then, Andrin's own work has landed on `main`:
  - the turnaround sheets and the runtime visual language (`docs/art/discoverables/`, `src/world/life/visuals.ts`, `src/world/artifacts.ts`);
  - fixed Logbook photos for all 36 entries (`docs/art/logbook-photos/`, `src/discovery/photos.ts`).
- The Predecessor's Mark on the pagoda says the older robot "kept going, further into the city". The next blocks follow it out of Old Town.
- `13_CITY_AND_DISTRICTS.md` lists the Flooded District, the Underground Metro and the Financial District but leaves them blank. This proposal fills in those three, one block each.

## The route

Old Town (Temple Hill) → **Block 3: Flooded Arcade** → **Block 4: Sunken Station** → **Block 5: Tower Foot**

Each block connects to the last through a passage where the blocks swap, like the stair lane. Only one block is ever drawn, so the frame rate stays about what one block costs. Each block gets its own Relay Station, its own PR and its own playable link. I'd build them in order, one at a time.

## Block 3: Flooded Arcade (edge of Old Town into the Flooded District)

**Way in:** behind the temple hall, a collapsed section of the compound wall opens onto a slope down to the water.

- **What it was:** a covered shopping arcade, a long glass-roofed street of small shops.
- **What it is now:** half the glass roof has fallen in, and the street is flooded knee-deep. It is a warm, bright wetland under a broken roof, with reeds, lilies and fish between the shop fronts.
- **Getting around:**
  - The robot can wade in shallow water, but slowly and at an energy cost.
  - Deep channels can't be crossed on foot. Fallen awnings, shop counters and the arcade's roof girders make a dry route above the water.
  - The first block where the high route is the fast route.
- **Landmark:** the arcade's big clock. It has stopped, and its face is cracked, and it hangs over the flooded crossing in the middle.
- **Relay Station 3:** on the arcade roof.

**New species (6)**

| Name | What it is | Completes when you… |
|---|---|---|
| Glasswort Reed | reed bed that grows on fallen roof glass | scan it on the glass where it roots |
| Gutter Lily | floating lily whose leaves close when the water is disturbed | wait still until the leaves reopen |
| Shopfront Moss | thick moss mats on the shop shutters | scan a shutter it has pulled open |
| Wader Heron | tall grey heron that fishes in the arcade | watch it catch a fish |
| Mudskipper Shoal | small fish that climb out onto the counters | scan them out of the water at low sun |
| **Snapping Eel** (danger) | lurks in the deep channels | see below |

**Danger: Snapping Eel.** A long eel in the deep water.
- **Warning:** ripples and a dorsal fin cut across the channel.
- **What it does:** if the robot wades into its channel, it snaps and drags the robot back to the bank. That costs energy and loses your place. It is never deadly.
- **How to avoid it:** take the high route, or cross while it is chasing fish.
- **Completes when:** you watch it from above.

**Major discovery: the Flood Line.** On the arcade's main pillar, people marked the rising water day by day with dates and short notes. The last mark is near the roof. It's the first hint that the city flooded slowly, not all at once.

**Smaller entries:**
- Locations: Arcade Entrance, Clock Crossing, Arcade Roof.
- Artifacts: shop shutter, arcade clock, rental bicycle rack, a child's boat.
- Origin: the Predecessor Unit's track marks, leading down the stairs into the station.

## Block 4: Sunken Station (Underground Metro)

**Way in:** the arcade's station stairs go down under the water line. The water has drained out of the lower levels.

- **What it was:** an underground metro station, with a ticket hall, platforms and a stopped train.
- **What it is now:** dark, cool and quiet. Daylight only gets in through shafts where the street above collapsed, and roots hang into the light. It's the first underground block.
- **Glow:** no glowing animals or plants, to keep Andrin's rule that biological glow is only for the Wisteria and the moth. The darkness is broken by light shafts and the robot's own scanner lens.
- **Getting around:**
  - The stopped train is a walkway: through the carriages, then over the roofs.
  - Escalators have turned into ramps.
  - A maintenance tunnel loops around the platform.
- **Landmark:** a huge root column grown down through a light shaft, standing on the platform like a tree.
- **Relay Station 4:** in the ticket hall.

**New species (6)**

| Name | What it is | Completes when you… |
|---|---|---|
| Root Curtain | pale roots hanging from the ceiling, reaching for water | scan where they touch the drip pools |
| Shaft Fern | ferns that grow only in light shafts | scan one at noon, in direct light |
| Cave Swift | small birds nesting in the tunnel mouth | watch the flock leave at dusk |
| Ticket Beetle | flat beetles living in the old ticket machines | scan one coming out of a machine slot |
| Pale Crayfish | blind crayfish in the drip pools | find them in the dark pool, away from light |
| **Tunnel Hound** (danger) | territorial predator in the dark tunnels | see below |

**Danger: Tunnel Hound.** A lean, eyeless animal that hunts by sound.
- **Warning:** a low clicking from the dark.
- **What it does:** sprinting or landing hard attracts it. It charges and knocks the robot down, which costs energy.
- **How to avoid it:** walk, don't run, in its tunnel, or go over the train roofs. This is the Old Town threat, "territorial predators in dark interiors", in its full form.
- **Completes when:** you watch it sleep in its den.

**Major discovery: the Last Departure board.** The station's departure board stopped on one final train, marked "Evacuation, all stations". The board and notices around it say people left in an orderly way. It changes how the Flood Line reads.

**Smaller entries:**
- Locations: Ticket Hall, Platform, Stopped Train, Maintenance Tunnel.
- Artifacts: ticket machine, station map, umbrella stand, lost-and-found shelf.
- Origin: a Predecessor Unit charging point, wired into the station's power line. It ran here for a long time.

## Block 5: Tower Foot (Financial District edge)

**Way in:** the maintenance tunnel ends in a tower's basement and climbs up into its lobby.

- **What it was:** the foot of a skyscraper, with its lobby, plaza and the first floors of offices.
- **What it is now:** the plaza is a young forest. The tower is split open on one side, and plants climb its floors.
- **Getting around:** the first vertical block. You climb broken floors, ducts and a fallen crane up about 30 m to a canopy terrace.
- **Landmark:** the tower itself, the first skyscraper up close. From the terrace you see the rest of the skyline, which is where the city goes next.
- **Relay Station 5:** on the terrace.

**New species (6)**

| Name | What it is | Completes when you… |
|---|---|---|
| Window Birch | thin birch trees rooted in office floors | scan one growing through a desk |
| Duct Vine | vine that grows up air ducts and pushes them apart | scan where it has split a duct |
| Lobby Fig | strangler fig wrapped around the lobby columns | scan it from the top of the column |
| Glass Swallow | birds that nest on window ledges | watch them dive past the facade |
| Plaza Deer | small deer grazing the plaza forest | approach without startling them |
| **Ledge Kite** (danger) | raptor guarding its nest high on the tower | see below |

**Danger: Ledge Kite.** A large bird of prey nesting on the tower.
- **Warning:** it circles and calls.
- **What it does:** getting close to its nest makes it swoop and knock the robot off the ledge. It costs a fall, not a life.
- **How to avoid it:** climb the other side of the tower, or pass while it hunts over the plaza.
- **Completes when:** you scan the nest while it's away.

**Major discovery: the Board Room.** A conference room on the top floor, with a city model on the table showing a sea wall that was never finished. It's the first look at the plan that failed.

**Smaller entries:**
- Locations: Plaza, Lobby, Split Floors, Canopy Terrace.
- Artifacts: revolving door, lobby directory, elevator car, city model.
- Origin: the Predecessor Unit itself, shut down at the terrace edge and looking at the skyline. That ends this chapter.
