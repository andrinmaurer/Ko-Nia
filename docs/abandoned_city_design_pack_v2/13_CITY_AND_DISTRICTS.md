# City and Districts

## Resolved Direction

The city is a fictional East Asian megacity containing:
- skyscrapers
- dense infrastructure
- older religious / cultural architecture
- shrines
- torii gates
- temples
- statues
- underground transit
- modern and futuristic remnants

Nature has transformed all of it after 2,000 years.

## District 1

The MVP block (Shrine Street) sits in this district.

Name: Old Town (working name)
Original purpose: historic shopping and temple quarter; narrow streets, small shops, shrines
Current ecosystem: damp, shaded street forest; vines on every façade, wetland puddles in the streets, insects and small scavengers
Visual identity: low wooden and concrete buildings, torii gates, statues, faded shop signs, hanging wisteria
Traversal: tight streets and alleys, collapsed overpasses and rooftops as an elevated layer
Key species: Lantern Wisteria, Hookbill, Drainback, Lantern Moth, Vault Mantis
Landmark: a large temple complex (beyond the MVP block); in the MVP, the neighborhood shrine
Threat: territorial predators in dark interiors
Mystery: the Predecessor Unit, an older robot like the player's own
