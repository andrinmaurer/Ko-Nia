# Scanning and Logbook

## Resolved Design Defaults

- The Logbook is the main progression system.
- First scan creates a page.
- Repeated observations reveal more information.
- Knowledge itself is the reward.
- No generic `+250 XP` style feedback.
- Unknown organisms begin unidentified.
- Species pages can link to related species and ecological relationships.
- Mapping unlocks at 5% total Logbook completion.

## Core Scanning Feel

Should scanning feel:
- effortless
- investigative
- deliberate
- scientific
- puzzle-like
- highly visual

Answer: highly visual yet effortless like taking a screenshot.

## Scanner Activation

- Hold button
- Toggle

- Combination

## First Contact Flow

Resolved baseline:

1. Player notices something unusual.
2. Scanner marks it as `UNKNOWN ORGANISM`.
3. Initial scan captures appearance and location.
4. A new Logbook page is created.
5. The page contains many unknown fields.
6. Further observations progressively fill it in.

Answer: there is a limited amount of discoverable animals, plants etc, each item counts toward 100%

## 5% Mapping Unlock

Resolved behavior:

At 5% total Logbook completion, the robot unlocks / reconstructs its mapping subsystem.

Before 5%, player gets only:
- heading
- relative position
- elevation
- Relay Station signals

After 5%, map shows only explored terrain.

Keep / change: keep
