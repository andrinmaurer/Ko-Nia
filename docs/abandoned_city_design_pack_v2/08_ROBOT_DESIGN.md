# Robot Design

## Resolved Design Defaults

- The player controls a highly advanced robotic explorer.
- The robot boots for the first time at the beginning of the game.
- It does not know who created it or why it exists.
- It does not speak, but can make expressive mechanical / electronic sounds.
- It does not become a traditional rebellious AI; the mystery is its origin, not whether it will disobey.
- Core traversal eventually includes walking, sprinting, climbing, grappling, hovering and gliding.
- Energy comes from solar charging and relay stations.
- Energy should constrain exploration without becoming repetitive survival micromanagement.

## Energy Model

Resolved principle:

> Energy is there to make the player think about where they go, not to make them babysit a battery meter.

Define:

- Base battery endurance: 500W (1m distance traveled = 10W)
- Solar charging while moving: Yes
- Relay Station full recharge: Yes
- Weather affects charging: Yes
- Night affects charging: Yes
- Hovering energy cost: 1.5x
- Grappling energy cost: 2x
- Scanner energy cost: 15W
- Thermal / night vision energy cost: 25W
- Underground exploration risk: 1.25x
