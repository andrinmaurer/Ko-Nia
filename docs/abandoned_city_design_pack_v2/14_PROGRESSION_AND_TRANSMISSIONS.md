# Progression and Transmissions

## Resolved Progression Philosophy

- Knowledge is the main reward.
- There is no conventional combat XP.
- Exploration upgrades expand possibility rather than simply increasing numbers.
- Every 5% total Logbook completion triggers a transmission from the robot's origin.
- These transmissions gradually reveal the home planet / colony and the robot's origin.

## What Counts Toward Completion?

- species discovered: 100% this
