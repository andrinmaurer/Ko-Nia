## Failure State

What happens if the robot is destroyed or disabled?

Examples:
- respawn at relay station
- deploy replacement body
- recover lost data
- permanent loss of field samples
- no traditional death mechanic

Describe: The Player respawns at a relay station, there should be multiple of those and reaching one marks it as the respawn point, since there is no linear story; returning to another savepoint marks that as the respawn point.
