## One-Sentence Pitch
A browser-based exploration game where the player controls a robot sent from a distant alien colony to investigate the ruins of an abandoned human city, now reclaimed by mutated plants and animals 2,000 years after humanity destroyed itself in an atomic war.

## Player Fantasy
What should the player feel like they are *being*?

Examples:
- a lonely scientific explorer
- an alien archaeologist
- a field biologist
- a machine slowly uncovering the truth about humanity
- a vulnerable outsider in an ecosystem that does not understand it

Your answer: I want to lean into the idea of a robotic explorer that was sent here to explore the world and catalogue everything. since it is the first time that this robot boots up or comes to life he does not yet know what he is, does or who created him. 

## Core Experience
What should players spend most of their time doing?

Examples:
- wandering
- observing wildlife
- scanning species
- photographing / cataloguing discoveries
- climbing ruined structures
- solving environmental mysteries
- collecting samples
- surviving dangerous encounters
- reconstructing historical events

Your answer: its an open world game, the player will spend most of the time exploring, scanning, collecting and surviving the harsh enviroment.

## Main Design Pillars
Pick 3–5 principles that every major feature should support.

Example:
1. Exploration over combat
2. Discovery should feel meaningful
3. The city should feel huge and mostly empty, yet overgrown and full of little insects and remnants
4. Nature should feel alien but biologically plausible
5. Environmental storytelling should replace exposition

Your pillars: 
1. Exploration over combat
2. Discovery should feel meaningful
3. The city should feel huge and mostly empty, yet overgrown and full of little insects and remnants
4. Nature should feel alien but biologically plausible

## Target Player
Who is this game for?

Examples:
- players who enjoy Outer Wilds
- Subnautica players
- environmental exploration fans
- slow-game / atmospheric-game players
- completionists who enjoy cataloguing

Your answer: Neurospicy (ADHD/OCD/Autism) but everybody can play and enjoy this game.

## Target Session Length
How long should a normal play session feel good for?

- 10–20 minutes
- 30–60 minutes
- 1–2 hours
- open-ended

Your answer: it should be possible to do quick sessions of 10-15 minutes but also bingeable so players can lose themselves and relax.

## Win / End Condition
Does the game have:
- a traditional ending?
- a final discovery?
- multiple endings?
- no ending, only exploration?
- a story campaign plus open exploration?

Describe: With each 5% of progress made (catalouging everything) the player learns more about its home planet trough transmissions sent from its home planet.

## What This Game Is NOT
List things you explicitly do not want.

Examples:
- not a shooter
- not a survival crafting grind
- not a roguelike
- not a linear story game
- not combat-focused

Your answer:
- Definitly not a shooter, the robot will not fight, its only option is fleeing.
- Not linear story, the bot can discover the world at its own pace and go whereever feels right.
- Not combat focused.
