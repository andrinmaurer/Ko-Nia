# Ko-Nia — discoverable asset blockout and turnaround brief

Status: written blockout complete; all 18 entries have a visually reviewed first-pass reference sheet. Three environment sheets have revised v2 selections.

## Source and scope

Based on [Ko-Nia](https://github.com/andrinmaurer/Ko-Nia), reviewed at commit `8ed0dfec262715add23498d26700400d016ee677`, particularly the [MVP specification](https://github.com/andrinmaurer/Ko-Nia/blob/8ed0dfec262715add23498d26700400d016ee677/docs/abandoned_city_design_pack_v2/17_MVP_SPECIFICATION.md) and `src/discovery/entries.ts`.

All 18 current discoverable entries: 5 flora, 4 fauna, 4 human artifacts, 1 Origin object and 4 locations. Relay Station is an interaction hub with an existing reference, not an additional Logbook entry. Future species and procedural districts are outside this inventory.

## Visual direction

Use the local KO-NIA_Block.png, KO-NIA_Bot.png and KO-NIA_Relay-Station.png as visual references. Weathered stylized realism, concrete and timber reclaimed by lush vegetation; credible mutated anatomy; quiet beauty and mystery. Biology has no mechanical parts. Cyan technology belongs to colony machinery; biological glow is limited to the Wisteria and moth relationship. Keep silhouettes distinct and readable at game distance. These sheets are concept/modeling references, not finished 3D assets.

The source describes species appearances and some world dimensions. All additional measurements below are **proposed art blockout dimensions**, subject to later gameplay fit. Older robot derivation is an art proposal grounded in the existing robot sheet. Reference art that implies larger environments or different relay lore does not override the authored MVP.

## Sheet format

One sheet per entry. Species/objects: matching front, side, back and top or three-quarter views, plus anatomy/material close-ups and relevant behavior states. Locations: consistent plan/elevation/cutaway views and one atmospheric perspective. Pale grey paper, restrained graphite labels and clear scale cues. Show the same design across panels. Supporting assets appear in their parent sheets.

## 01. Lantern Wisteria

- **Shape/material blockout:** Hanging woody vine with cascading pale violet flowers and translucent papery lantern-shaped seed pods. Tangled bark stems, pinnate wisteria leaves; soft blue-green pod glow only at night.

- **Placement:** Overpass underside.
- **Discovery:** First scan; close Biological scan; night glow observation.

## Visual review and modeling handoff

- All 18 entries are covered with individual sheets. Plant and animal behavior states, object construction details and environment views are present.
- Revised Shrine Street removes the transit train and fixes the landmark key; revised Noodle Shop shows a mantis ootheca and daylight-only interior; revised Overpass replaces the erroneous pod with the seated Predecessor Unit.
- These are first-pass concept turnarounds, not orthographic CAD or approved final models. Some panel angles are approximate and fine details drift across views.
- In the early flora, Hookbill and moth sheets, robot icons are illustrative, separately scaled references. Do not infer species size from their drawn relationship. Use the proposed measurements in this file.
- Noodle Shop's bottom-right silhouette still incorrectly suggests 9 m height: 12 × 9 m is its floor footprint. Do not model a 9 m tall ground-floor room.
- The Menu Board render is taller than its proposed 90 × 65 cm proportion. Model to the written dimensions or explicitly revise that art decision.
- Shrine Street's overview emphasizes atmosphere over survey accuracy. Use the written block dimensions and gameplay routes for layout; the isolated Overpass and Shrine sheets govern those component designs.
- The Predecessor's older-model identity is established, but joints/panels should be reconciled against the existing player robot when modeled.
- Keep species geometry and texture detail within the browser game's performance budget. The painted material richness is a visual target, not a requirement to model every leaf or scratch.
